# emulated VSA

### what?
To implement an VSA Mechanism into the Motor Controller as part of the torque control

### where is the idea from
This concept was already used in the design of the [[MIT Cheetah]] and is widely spread among current robotic platforms. It and the MIT Cheetah are in need for further reseach!

### why
This is possible due to the [[backdriveability]] of Tobias Reich's motors. Because the motor controller is controlling at speed's above 10khz it feels as direct as mechanical springs.
The limitation of the idea lies in the instantaneous reality of mechanical shocks. And in the energy costs because there is no physical energy save. But it is implementable with comparatively low effort and shines with zero additional build volume.
