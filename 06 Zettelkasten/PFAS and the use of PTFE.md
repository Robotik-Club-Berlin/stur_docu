# PFAS and the use of PTFE

### what?
PTFE part of the PFAS group, known as "Forever Chemicals"
thus a different Material must be chosen to enable the abitlity 
to recreate the Hugo Arm long-term. 


### where?
It is important to think about it for the whole design process of the arm.
Because of the possibility for regulation to ban the use of PFAS as a whole. 

### how?
One way the resolve the issue is to use pfas free liner technology's 
through the motor axis and an ball bearing pulley for changing direction
to reduce the added friction that comes with PFAS-free liners.
